§01 About

Anushka is a robotics engineer focused on getting autonomous systems out of simulation and onto real hardware. Her work spans the full stack of a manipulation pipeline — RGB‑D perception, TF transforms, motion planning, and closed‑loop control — validated in Gazebo and Isaac Sim before deployment on physical robots like the Hello Robot Stretch RE3.

Alongside manipulation, she works on SLAM and state estimation (robust pose‑graph optimization, Kalman‑filtered sensor fusion for AUV navigation), and on assistive and human‑robot‑interaction systems — including a wearable that gives hearing‑impaired users directional awareness of oncoming vehicles via Doppler‑based audio processing.

Currently an embedded systems intern at Tatum Robotics in Boston, working on human‑to‑device interaction pipelines for assistive input hardware.
